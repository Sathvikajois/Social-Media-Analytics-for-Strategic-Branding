## SOCIAL MEDIA ANALYTICS FOR STRATEGIC BRANDING

## Project Overview:
This project analyzes Tesla’s social media performance across platforms such as Twitter (X), Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn. The goal was to identify engagement trends, campaign effectiveness, follower growth patterns, and content strategies using Excel analytics and dashboards.

## The project focuses on helping Tesla improve:
--> Brand engagement
--> Campaign ROI
--> Audience growth
--> Content strategy
--> Platform-specific performance

## OBJECTIVES:
--> Analyze engagement metrics across platforms
--> Identify top-performing content and hashtags
--> Compare ad spend vs engagement
--> Evaluate campaign effectiveness
--> Visualize follower growth trends
--> Provide data-driven marketing recommendations

## TOOLS USED:
--> Microsoft Excel
--> Pivot Tables
--> Charts & Graphs
--> Data Cleaning
--> Dashboarding
--> Conditional Formatting

## KEY ANALYSIS PERFORMED:
Data Cleaning:
--> Removed duplicates
--> Standardized dates and platform names
--> Formatted numerical columns
--> Split hashtags into individual tags
Engagement Analysis:
--> Calculated engagement rate
--> Identified top-performing posts
--> Analyzed content-type performance
--> Compared hashtag effectiveness
Platform Analysis:
--> Compared follower growth
--> Analyzed engagement vs ad spend
--> Identified best-performing platforms
Campaign Effectiveness:
--> Measured ROI of campaigns
--> Evaluated engagement uplift
--> Analyzed follower retention trends

--> Video content generated the highest engagement on YouTube
--> Twitter performed best for product announcements
--> Campaign-specific hashtags increased interaction significantly
--> Higher ad spend correlated with stronger follower growth during launches
